# The association's banners and logo

Suomen eOppimiskeskus ry's own pictures, which the newsletter templates start
from, so an editor does not add them to every email:

- `uutiskirje.png`: the green banner of the newsletter (Uutiskirje), 1128 × 222,
  as it is in the newsletters in the association's public Mailchimp archive.
- `jasenkirje.png`: the magenta banner of the member letter (Jäsenkirje),
  1128 × 222, from the member letter the association gave us.
- `eoppimiskeskus.png`: the association's logo with its name, 304 × 60 with a
  see-through background, from the same member letter.

They are the association's, used in its own newsletters. Nothing else from the
member letter is copied here: it goes to members only.

They are the defaults. An admin can put other pictures in their place on
Asetukset (Banneri ja logo) and go back to these whenever they like; new
emails start with what is chosen there.

The banners are 1128 pixels wide, more than the email's 660, so they stay
sharp on phone screens.
