# Social icons

The icons the newsletter's Social block shows, 96 pixels square with see-through
corners, in three styles: the network's own colour (`-color`), dark grey
(`-dark`) and white for dark backgrounds (`-light`).

The signs of Facebook, Instagram, X, YouTube, TikTok and Bluesky are from
[Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org/) 13.21, released under CC0 1.0 (public
domain). The names and logos stay their owners' trademarks; using them to link
to the association's own pages is what the networks' brand guidelines allow.
LinkedIn's "in", the globe and the envelope are drawn for this project.

They are PNG because many email programs, Outlook and Gmail among them, do not
show SVG.
