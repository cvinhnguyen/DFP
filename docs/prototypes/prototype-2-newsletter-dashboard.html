Fields on the dashboard, and where each one comes from

The editors agreed this list. Every field comes from a part of the system that already exists, so nothing on the screen is promised that we cannot actually produce.

FieldWhat the editor seesProduced by
TitleHeadline of the article, links to the originalCrawler
PublisherOrganisation that published it, named on every entryCrawler
SourceFeed or site the item was collected fromCrawler
Publication dateDate given by the publisherCrawler
Original linkAddress of the article itselfCrawler
SectionWhich part of the newsletter it belongs in: events, member news, highlights from the fieldEditor, for now
AI summaryThree to four sentences, one click away on the rowSummarisation workflow
Detected signalsTopic tags such as tekoäly or saavutettavuusSignal workflow
Selection stateWhether the item is going into this newsletterDashboard
Duplicate markerPoints at the item this one repeatsDashboard
AuthorRequested by the editors, but left out of the designNobody

Author was checked with the crawler owner: most of the sources do not publish a machine-readable author, so the field would usually be empty. It is recorded here as a known gap rather than built into the dashboard. Section is stored on every item, but nothing fills it in automatically yet, so for now an editor picks it.
